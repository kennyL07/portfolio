About this project

This was my very exciting first year doing robotics with FRC team 2158. Coming into robotics, I had never coded in a team before. I was pretty scared that my skills would be lack luster so I put off joining for 2 months until my friends convinced me to join.

The game this year was called OVERCHARGED, where the objective was to pick up cones or cubes across the field on the enemy side and place them on your designated holders. At the end, you would balance on the see-saw like platform with multiple robots getting more points.

This year I learned Java, Github project management skills, and how to work in a team. I worked on an LED subsystem for the robot working with 3 different drivers and both LED Matrixs + Strips, a time-of-flight sensor for the intake subsystem, and auto-balancing using a Proportional-controller and the built in gyroscope on the robot.
