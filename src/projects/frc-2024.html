About this project

This year, I focused more on VEX than FRC as I was the lead programmer for their team. While I was in VEX, I learned a lot about motor control and localization. Bringing that knowledge into FRC, I worked on automatic aiming, and PID tuning.

This robot had a really ambitious design, but delivery was a difficulty and our subsystems broke down. Although a lot of programming innovation was made, we had bigger issues to handle.
